**Do I need a trainer?** No. Choose the virtual trainer and NARUZU simulates power, heart rate and cadence. Those rides are marked simulated and cannot set your FTP.

**Which trainers work?** Bluetooth trainers that use the standard FTMS protocol are the target. Real-trainer behaviour is not yet verified with this build, and no brand is declared compatible.

**Does it support ANT+?** No.

**Does it work on iPhone?** Not yet verified. Safari cannot pair trainers.

**Where are my rides stored?** On your device. Desktop data is in `%APPDATA%\NARUZU` (Windows) or `~/.config/NARUZU` (Linux). Browser data is separate.

**Can I move rides to another computer?** Use **Export full backup** and **Restore full backup** in Settings, Data and accounts. Credentials are not included.

**Which units are used?** Metric (km, kg) by default; switch to imperial in Settings, Rider.

**Why is my FTP marked Estimated?** It is the default guess. Do the [ramp test](/naruzu-web/docs/user/ramp-test-and-ftp/) or type your own.

**Does the app update itself?** No. Install a newer version over the old one; back up first.

**Does it work offline?** Yes for simulation, local routes, workouts, recording and export once installed or cached. New Quick Ride searches need internet. See [Offline use](/naruzu-web/docs/user/offline-use/).

**Can I export to Strava or Garmin?** Export a FIT file and import it on the site. Direct upload is optional and live uploads are not verified.

**Is there career mode, racing, the Shop or Companions?** Not in the public beta.

**Why does loading take so long?** The first load compiles graphics programs; see [launch time](/naruzu-web/docs/user/setup-and-launch/#how-long-does-loading-take-an-honest-answer).

**Is my data shared?** Nothing is shared until you choose. See [Privacy and your data](/naruzu-web/docs/user/privacy-and-data/).

_Last verified against version 0.1.0-beta.1._
