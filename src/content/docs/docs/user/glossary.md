title: Glossary
description: Plain-language definitions of FTP, ERG, FTMS and other terms used in NARUZU.

---

**Cadence.** Pedal revolutions per minute (rpm).

**Calibration (spin-down).** A trainer routine where you pedal up to a speed and then coast so the trainer can measure its own friction.

**Critical power (CP) and W-prime.** Two numbers for an estimate of how much work you can do above CP. NARUZU shows W-prime balance only when you set both yourself.

**ERG.** A mode where the trainer sets resistance to hold your target watts, whatever your cadence.

**Estimated FTP.** A default or guessed FTP, not a measurement. Marked with an Estimated badge.

**Flat fallback.** A route file with no elevation data is ridden as flat terrain.

**FIT file.** A common activity file format that sports sites import. NARUZU exports your ride as FIT.

**FTMS.** Fitness Machine Service, the standard Bluetooth protocol smart trainers use for data and control.

**FTP.** Functional threshold power: the most power, in watts, you could hold for about an hour. Zones and workout targets come from it.

**GPX file.** A common route file format.

**Grade.** The steepness of the road, in percent.

**HUD.** The heads-up display of live numbers over the ride.

**Normalized power.** A smoothed power figure that weights hard efforts more. Provisional under five minutes or with gaps.

**Power zones.** Ranges of power, shown as Z1 to Z7 style markers, set from your FTP.

**Quick Ride.** A generated real-road loop near a place you choose.

**Ramp test.** A test with one-minute steps that rise until you cannot continue; your FTP estimate is 75 percent of your best minute.

**Virtual shifting.** Experimental gear shifting on the trainer without changing your real gear.

**Virtual trainer.** A simulated trainer that makes up power, heart rate and cadence.

**W/kg.** Power divided by body weight.

_Last verified against version 0.1.0-beta.1._
